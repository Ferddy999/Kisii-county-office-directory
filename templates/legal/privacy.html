Privacy and Cookie Policy

County Office Directory, County Government of Kisii. Last updated {{ legal.updated }}.

1. Who we are

The County Government of Kisii runs this system and is the data controller for the personal data in it. We handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

2. What we collect and why

DataWhy we hold it
Account details: username, name, email address, phone number and your sign-in code preferenceTo create your account, confirm it is you with a one-time code and contact you about your account.
Directory records: officers’ names, designations, work phone numbers and work email addresses, with their offices and departmentsTo let users find the right officer or office. This is part of the county’s public service work.
Audit records: who changed which record, when and what changed, and sign-in activityTo keep the system secure and accountable.
Consent records: when you accepted the Terms and your cookie choicesTo show we asked for and respected your choices.

We do not collect more than we need, and one-time codes are not stored in readable form and expire after five minutes.

3. Who can see it

Directory records are visible to signed-in users. Administrators can change them. Account details and audit records are visible only to administrators. We do not sell personal data. We share it outside the county only where the law requires.

4. How long we keep it

Directory records are kept while the officer and office are active and are removed or corrected on request through the ICT office. Account details are kept while the account exists. Audit and consent records are kept for as long as needed for security and accountability, as set by county policy.

5. How we protect it

Access needs a password and a one-time code. Passwords are stored in hashed form. Permissions limit what each user can do, and changes are logged.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to how it is used and withdraw consent you have given. Contact the county ICT office at {{ legal.contact_email }}. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. We use them as follows.

CookieTypePurposeLasts
sessionidEssentialKeeps you signed inUntil you close the browser, or 8 hours
csrftokenEssentialProtects forms against forgeryUp to 1 year
kcd_consentEssentialRemembers your cookie choice180 days
messagesEssentialShows a confirmation message onceUntil shown

Usage and performance cookies are optional. None are in use today. If they are introduced, we will only set them if you have chosen Accept all, and we will update this policy.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Cookie settings. You can also clear cookies in your browser, but you will then be signed out.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and show the date above.
