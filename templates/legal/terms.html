1. About this system

The County Office Directory is an internal system of the County Government of Kisii. It lists county departments, directorates, offices, services and the officers who work in them, so that staff and authorised users can find the right office and contact details quickly.

2. Who may use it

The system is for authorised county staff and other persons the county ICT office has given an account. You must not use an account that was not issued to you. Administrators can add, change and delete records. Ordinary users can only view and search.

3. Your account and sign-in

Keep your password private and do not share the one-time codes we send to your email or phone. Tell the county ICT office at once if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for activity carried out under your account.

4. Acceptable use

  • Use directory information only for county business.
  • Do not copy, sell, publish or pass on directory data to anyone outside the county without authority.
  • Do not try to break into, overload or interfere with the system, or to see data you are not permitted to see.
  • Administrators must enter accurate information and change records only as part of their duties.

5. Accuracy of information

We try to keep records correct, but offices and officers change. Where accuracy matters, confirm details with the office concerned.

6. Monitoring and audit

Changes to records are logged with the user, the time and what changed. Sign-ins are also recorded. These logs are used to keep the system secure and accountable.
